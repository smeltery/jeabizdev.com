# Find your way around

Use the main navigation to explore services, John’s background, JEA’s approach,
partnering opportunities, and the business development toolkit. On a phone, open
**Menu** for the same links and a contact action at the bottom of the screen.

## Search

Choose the search icon or press **Cmd+K** on macOS or **Ctrl+K** elsewhere.
Search by topic, target, asset name, or tool name. Category buttons narrow the
results to pages, tools, assets, or transactions.

- Type a phrase such as “KAT6,” “royalties,” or “partnering.”
- Use the up/down arrow keys to move through results, then Enter to open one.
- Press Escape or select the close button to return to the page.

Search runs in your browser. No search account is needed.

## Resources and common questions

**Resources** contains the transaction track record, downloadable deal summaries,
and the existing Google Drive reference libraries. External libraries open in a
new tab; access depends on their sharing settings.

FAQs appear on the home and approach pages. Select a question to expand its
answer. Opening another question closes the previous one. Questions also work
with Tab and Enter or Space.

The footer contains direct contact links, privacy information, the disclaimer,
brand downloads, and a back-to-top action.

## Motion and accessibility

Pages use a brief fade and blur transition, and search and mobile navigation ease
in and out while preserving keyboard focus. Back to top uses a short eased scroll
that stops when you scroll, touch, or press a key. Reduced-motion preferences
disable these effects and the contact-form celebration.
